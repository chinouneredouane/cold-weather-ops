Module 1 · Wind and Limitations

Lesson 01: Wind Reports and Wind Components

Lesson 1 of 3 · about 6 min

Learning objectives

  • Interpret wind reports correctly.
  • Calculate headwind, tailwind and crosswind components.
  • Use gusts correctly.

Wind reports

  • In the METAR, the wind is a 10-minute mean; in reports for take-off and landing from the tower (and ATIS), it is a 2-minute mean for the runway (ICAO Annex 3).
  • Gusts are reported when the maximum wind exceeds the mean by 10 kt or more: 27015G28KT = 270°, 15 kt, gusting 28 kt.
  • Variable direction: 27015KT 240V300 — direction varies between 240° and 300°.
  • Directions in METAR / TAF are true; tower and ATIS winds are magnetic.

Components

  • Use the FMS / EFB, the wind component chart or the rule of thumb.
  • For limits, check the crosswind with the gust and the tailwind with the steady wind and the gust as your procedure states.
  • With a variable wind direction, use the most unfavourable direction.

Key takeaways

  • METAR wind: 10-min mean, true; tower / ATIS: 2-min mean, magnetic.
  • Gusts reported when 10 kt or more above the mean.
  • Crosswind ≈ wind × sin(angle); use the gust for crosswind limits.

Knowledge check

Wind 090°/20 kt on runway 06. Approximately what is the crosswind?

  1. 5 kt
  2. 10 kt
  3. 17 kt
  4. 20 kt

Answer: B. 30° off the runway: half the wind, about 10 kt.
