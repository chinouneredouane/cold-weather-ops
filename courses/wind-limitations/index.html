Purpose

Refresh all flight crews' knowledge of wind reports and wind limitations, and of the application of the company limits in the OM B, FCOM / AOM and AFM.

Who should take this course

All flight crew: Boeing 737-800, Dash 8-Q400 and Dash 8-Q200.

What you will be able to do

  • Interpret wind reports and calculate components
  • Distinguish demonstrated crosswind from limitations
  • Apply crosswind limits by runway condition
  • Apply the most restrictive company limit and decide
