Boeing 737-800 NG Performance

Take-off, en-route and landing performance of the Boeing 737-800 NG: speeds, limiting weights, runway condition, climb and obstacles, reduced thrust, cruise, driftdown and landing distance.

Purpose

Give B737-800 flight crew a complete understanding of aeroplane performance — the certification basis (CS-25), the operating rules (Part-CAT CAT.POL.A) and Boeing practice — so they can use the company performance tool, FCOM PI and QRH with full understanding.

Who should take this course

Boeing 737-800 NG flight crew (type rating and recurrent).

What you will be able to do

  • Define the certification and operational take-off speeds
  • Build the maximum allowable take-off weight
  • Apply field length, runway condition, climb and obstacle rules
  • Use reduced and derated thrust correctly
  • Choose cruise altitude and speed
  • Plan and fly a driftdown
  • Apply the dispatch and in-flight landing rules
