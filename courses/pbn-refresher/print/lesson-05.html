Module 2 · PBN Operations

Lesson 05: Approvals, MEL and Crew Responsibilities

Lesson 5 of 5 · about 5 min

Learning objectives

  • Identify the approvals and documents for PBN operations.
  • Apply the MEL to navigation equipment.
  • Summarise crew responsibilities in PBN operations.

Documents and approvals

  • The AFM states the aircraft's PBN capabilities. The OM B lists the company's PBN operations by fleet.
  • The flight plan item 10 and item 18 (PBN/) codes must match the aircraft's actual capability and approvals.
  • Specific approvals (for example RNP AR APCH) are listed in the operations specifications of the AOC.

MEL

The MEL includes the dispatch requirements for systems that need a specific capability or approval — RNAV/PBN, RVSM and LVO. Check the MEL for FMS, GNSS receivers, IRS and display units before planning a PBN route or approach.

Crew responsibilities

  1. Plan: NOTAMs, GNSS integrity, database currency, equipment and MEL.
  2. Load and cross-check procedures from the database.
  3. Fly with the required modes and monitor deviations and alerts.
  4. Act on alerts: inform ATC, apply the contingency, go around when required.
  5. Report navigation errors and GNSS interference.

Key takeaways

  • AFM + OM B + AOC specify PBN capability and approvals; flight plan codes must match.
  • Check the MEL for FMS, GNSS, IRS and displays.
  • Plan, load and check, fly and monitor, act on alerts, report.

Knowledge check

Where are specific approvals such as RNP AR APCH recorded?

  1. In the AFM only
  2. In the operations specifications of the AOC
  3. In the flight plan
  4. On the chart

Answer: B. Specific approvals appear in the operations specifications.
