Domestic Airlines — Global Reporting Format (GRF)
Lesson 9: Transmission of the Information — SNOWTAM, ATS and ATIS

Learning objectives:
- Describe the SNOWTAM rules (format, language, validity, replacement).
- Apply the SNOWTAM publication criteria.
- Describe how ATS and ATIS pass the RCR to pilots and how AIREPs come back.

The information flow

The aerodrome operator sends the RCR to the air traffic services and requests a SNOWTAM from the aeronautical information service. ATS pass the information to pilots by radio and ATIS ; pilots send AIREPs back through ATS.

SNOWTAM rules

Tap a card to turn it over.
SNOWTAM definition A special series NOTAM, in a standard format, that publishes the RCR — new format since the GRF (EU 2020/469). Language Issued in English (national language in addition if necessary). Validity 8 hours maximum. A new SNOWTAM is issued with each new RCR and replaces the previous one. Times Filing time is after the observation time — ideally as close as possible. Error Issue a new SNOWTAM and cancel the previous one; check the accuracy of what is published and inform the personnel concerned. Two SNOWTAMs? Never: the new one replaces the old one.

Example

SNOWTAM 0131, EADD: 02170055 09L 5/5/5 100/100/100 NR/NR/03 WET/WET/WET SNOW 02170135 09R 5/2/2 100/50/75 NR/06/06 WET/SLUSH/SLUSH

One SNOWTAM can contain the RCR of one or several runways, each with its own observation time.

When is a SNOWTAM published?

Runway Action

Contaminated (standing water, snow, slush, ice, frost) on at least part SNOWTAM via AIS and RCR to ATS

Dry, wet or slippery wet after removal or treatment of snow, slush, ice or frost SNOWTAM via AIS and RCR to ATS

Wet or slippery wet in other cases (e.g. a dry runway becomes wet) RCR to ATS (controller or AFIS) only

ATS and ATIS

ATS transmit the RCR information in the landing direction of the runway in use, by radio and on the ATIS.

Basic rule: when the runway is not dry, ATS request AIREPs from pilots.

Pilots report the braking action when it is equal to or worse than the reported condition.

Key takeaways:
- RCR → ATS (radio, ATIS); RCR → AIS → SNOWTAM.
- SNOWTAM: English, 8 h maximum, new one replaces old, filed after observation.
- Runway not dry → ATS request AIREPs.
