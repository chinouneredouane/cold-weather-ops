Global Reporting Format (GRF)

Runway surface condition assessment and reporting with the GRF: why it exists, contaminants, the RCAM and RWYCC, pilot reports, the RCR, SNOWTAM and ATS — and what it means for aircraft performance. Definitions as interactive flip cards.

Purpose

Explain the ICAO Global Reporting Format so that every actor — aerodrome operator, ATS, AIS and flight crews — understands the runway condition report and its transmission, following the company GRF syllabus (4 h 15) and completed with the flight crew performance perspective.

Who should take this course

Flight crew of all fleets (B737-800, Q400, Q200), flight operations and ground staff concerned by runway condition reporting.

What you will be able to do

  • Identify the objectives pursued by ICAO
  • Describe the main characteristics of the new system
  • Identify runway surface states and contaminants
  • Assign a RWYCC with the RCAM
  • Adjust the RWYCC with pilot reports
  • Write and update a runway condition report
  • Describe the SNOWTAM and the information flow
  • Use the RWYCC in the landing performance assessment
