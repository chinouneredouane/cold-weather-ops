Purpose

Refresh flight crew knowledge of RVSM operations in line with ICAO Doc 9574, PANS-ATM and EASA SPA.RVSM, as required for recurrent training.

Who should take this course

All flight crew. Full procedures apply to the Boeing 737-800; Q400 and Q200 crews take it for awareness of RVSM traffic, phraseology and ACAS II.

What you will be able to do

  • Define RVSM airspace and the required equipment
  • Plan and prepare an RVSM flight
  • Apply en-route height-keeping procedures and altimeter checks
  • Handle RVSM contingencies and special situations
  • Use ACAS II version 7.1 correctly in RVSM airspace
  • Use RVSM phraseology and reporting
