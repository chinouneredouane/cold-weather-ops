Upset Prevention and Recovery

Academic upset prevention and recovery training (UPRT): aerodynamics, causes of upsets, human factors, prevention and recovery techniques — aligned with ICAO Doc 10011.

Purpose

Provide Domestic Airlines flight crews with the knowledge needed to prevent, recognise and recover from aeroplane upsets, in line with the academic training elements of ICAO Doc 10011 and industry guidance.

Who should take this course

Flight crew (captains and first officers). Also suitable for instructors, examiners and flight operations staff.

What you will be able to do

  • Define upsets and explain the causes and contributing factors of loss of control
  • Apply the aerodynamics of angle of attack, stall, load factor and high-altitude flight
  • Manage energy, automation and flight path to prevent upsets
  • Recognise startle, surprise and spatial disorientation, and manage them
  • Use monitoring, callouts and intervention to stop a developing upset
  • Apply stall, nose-high and nose-low recovery principles
  • Explain the limits of simulator training and prepare for FSTD UPRT
