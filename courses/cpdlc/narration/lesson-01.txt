Domestic Airlines — CPDLC — Controller-Pilot Data Link
Lesson 1: CPDLC: Definition, References and Benefits

Learning objectives:
- Define CPDLC and describe its purpose.
- Identify the ICAO and European references.
- List the expected benefits and limitations.

Definition

Controller–Pilot Data Link Communications (CPDLC) is a means of communication between controller and pilot using data link for ATC communications (ICAO Doc 4444, PANS-ATM). It is a two-way system by which controllers can send non-urgent, strategic messages to an aircraft as an alternative to voice. The message is displayed on a flight deck display.

Controllers can issue clearances (levels, routes and deviations, speeds), frequency assignments and requests for information.

Pilots can respond to messages, request and receive clearances and information, and send reports.

A free text capability exchanges information that does not fit the standard formats.

A short history in Europe

The Data Link Services Implementing Rule (Regulation (EC) No 29/2009) mandated CPDLC above FL285 in European airspace. Its implementation was affected by technical problems — random disconnections called provider aborts . An EASA investigation found that they came from a combination of factors linked to the radio-frequency environment and the single-frequency data link infrastructure, and recommended a multi-frequency infrastructure. The dates of application were therefore deferred (Regulation (EU) 2015/310 and later amendments). In 2023 the requirements were consolidated in Regulation (EU) 2023/1770.

Benefits

Less voice traffic on the frequency and increased sector capacity;

more pilot requests handled at the same time;

fewer misunderstandings — for example call sign confusion;

safer frequency changes and fewer loss-of-communication events.

Limitation CPDLC latency is much longer than voice — a response can take up to several minutes. Use it only for non-time-critical communications. Check your aircraft limitations: on the B737 with protected-mode CPDLC (PM-CPDLC), the installation is intended for use in the cruise phase and for non-critical messages only.

Key takeaways:
- CPDLC: data link ATC messages for non-urgent, strategic communications.
- References: Annex 10 Vol III, PANS-ATM, ICAO GOLD (Doc 10037), EU Regulation 2023/1770.
- Benefits: less frequency congestion, fewer misunderstandings; limitation: latency — not for time-critical messages.
