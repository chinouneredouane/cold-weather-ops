CPDLC — Controller-Pilot Data Link

Controller-pilot data link communications: references, data link services, message principles, phraseology, failures and the B737 CMU-900.

Purpose

Give flight crews an operational understanding of CPDLC and its safety issues, in line with ICAO PANS-ATM and GOLD (Doc 10037) and current EU data link rules.

Who should take this course

All flight crew. CMU-900 operation applies to the Boeing 737-800; Dash 8 crews take it for awareness unless their aircraft is equipped.

What you will be able to do

  • Define CPDLC, its references and its benefits
  • Describe the data link services and the logon
  • Apply the principles of CPDLC exchanges and responses
  • Revert to voice and use CPDLC phraseology
  • Operate the CMU-900 CPDLC pages on the B737-800
  • Avoid common CPDLC errors
