Algerian Regulations and Duty Time

Algerian aviation law and the ANAC, differences from ICAO, flight procedures and fuel, and the duty time rules — with an interactive duty time calculator and practice exercises.

Purpose

Refresh flight crew knowledge of the Algerian regulation applicable to Domestic Airlines operations and train pilots to calculate duty time, WOCL reductions, extensions and rest.

Who should take this course

All flight crew: Boeing 737-800, Dash 8-Q400 and Dash 8-Q200.

What you will be able to do

  • Rank the Algerian legal texts and describe the role of the ANAC
  • Apply the Algerian differences from ICAO
  • Apply the flight procedures, night VFR and fuel rules
  • Calculate the maximum duty time period with legs and WOCL
  • Apply extensions, the commander's authority and waivers
  • Calculate rest periods
  • Use the duty time calculator
