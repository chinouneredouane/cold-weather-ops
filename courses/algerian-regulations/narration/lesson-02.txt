Domestic Airlines — Algerian Regulations and Duty Time
Lesson 2: Differences from ICAO Standards

Learning objectives:
- Find where the Algerian differences are published.
- Apply the main differences to Annexes 1, 2, 4, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13 and 15.
- Apply the differences to Doc 4444 and Doc 8400.

Where to find them

The differences from ICAO SARPs are published in AIP Algeria GEN 1.7 and in the Jeppesen Airway Manual (Air Traffic Control — State Rules and Procedures — Africa). Annex 6 (Operation of Aircraft): no difference. The national texts are on the ANAC website: www.anac.dz .

Annex 2 — Rules of the Air

Subject Algerian rule

Fitness to fly A crew member who feels unfit to fly shall not act as a pilot or crew member.

Minimum height Except over cities, assemblies of people or dense constructions: not below 150 m (500 ft) above ground, water or the highest obstacle.

IFR minimum height At least 1,500 ft (450 m) above the highest obstacle within 8 km (4.3 NM) of the estimated position.

Prohibited area entered Advise ATC, leave the area and land as soon as possible at the nearest aerodrome — or the aircraft may be forced to land by the military.

Flight plan Required for all IFR flights and for every flight (VFR or IFR) over desert areas and the sea .

ETD in the flight plan The time the aircraft is ready to leave the parking stand.

Other Annexes

Reference Algerian difference

Annex 1 — Licensing Algeria does not apply the multi-crew pilot licence (MPL) .

Annex 4 — Charts Some 1:500,000 charts published in Algeria do not conform to Chapter 10 and are not labelled ICAO.

Annex 9 — Facilitation No crew member certificate to the Appendix 7 model; crews not on regular international services need a valid passport and possibly a visa.

Annex 10 — Communications Conform to the SARPs; Annex 10 Volume II procedures apply with the Algerian authority.

Annex 11 — ATS Alerting (emergency phases) in the Algiers FIR follows a specific instruction, with delays adapted to desert and maritime areas.

Annex 12 — SAR Foreign SAR teams need a special permission.

Annexes 13 and 15 Applicable; some aeronautical publications may be without English translation; international NOTAMs are translated into English as far as possible.

Doc 8400 — Abbreviations QAN (wind direction) is referenced to true north ; QNH is never given in inches of mercury.

Doc 4444 — ATM Minimum obstacle clearance 1,500 ft (450 m) instead of 1,000 ft; special VFR not authorised below 1.5 km visibility (except helicopters); flight plan changes take effect only when acknowledged by ATC.

Key takeaways:
- Differences: AIP Algeria GEN 1.7 and Jeppesen State Rules.
- IFR minimum height 1,500 ft within 8 km; minimum height 500 ft.
- Flight plan for all IFR flights and any flight over desert or sea.
- No MPL in Algeria; QNH never in inches.
