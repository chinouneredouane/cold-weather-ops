Low Visibility Operations (LVO) — CAT II

Low visibility taxi, take-off and CAT II autoland on the Boeing 737-800: concepts, visual references, aerodrome lighting, company minima, procedures, callouts and failures.

Purpose

Train B737-800 crews in the company low visibility procedures (LV taxi, LVTO and CAT II autoland), based on the company All Weather Operation procedures, ICAO Doc 9365 and the ICAO low visibility presentation.

Who should take this course

Boeing 737-800 flight crew (captains and first officers). Q400 and Q200 crews may take it for awareness.

What you will be able to do

  • Explain AWO, LVO, LVP and the approach categories
  • Use visual references correctly at and below DH
  • Recognise LVO aerodrome lighting, stop bars and protected areas
  • Plan the flight and taxi in low visibility
  • Apply the LVTO minima and procedures
  • Fly the CAT II autoland with the company callouts
  • Handle failures and the go-around
