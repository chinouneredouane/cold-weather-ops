Hot Weather Operations

Summer and hot weather operations for all fleets: meteorology, density altitude and performance, take-off planning, brake energy, engines, obstacles, MEL, CRM, situation awareness, human performance and safety trends.

Purpose

Prepare all flight crews (B737-800, Q400 and Q200) to operate safely and efficiently in high temperatures, following the company hot weather syllabus, ICAO Annex 6 and EASA CS-25 / Part-CAT performance principles.

Who should take this course

All flight crew: Boeing 737-800, Dash 8-Q400 and Dash 8-Q200.

What you will be able to do

  • Recognise high temperature weather threats
  • Explain density altitude and its effect on performance
  • Plan high temperature take-offs with approved data
  • Manage brake energy and rejected take-offs
  • Respect engine limitations and prevent hot starts
  • Assess climb gradient and obstacle clearance
  • Apply MEL/CDL penalties in hot weather
  • Use CRM and situation awareness when margins are small
  • Manage heat stress and dehydration
  • Prevent the summer flight safety events
