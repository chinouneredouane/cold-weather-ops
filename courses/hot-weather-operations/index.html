Hot Weather Operations

Summer and hot weather operations for all fleets: hot-and-high performance, engines and systems, fuel, sand and convective weather, flight safety focus items.

Purpose

Prepare all flight crews (B737-800, Q400 and Q200) to operate safely, effectively and efficiently in a hot weather environment, as the ground part of the summer recurrent training.

Who should take this course

All flight crew: Boeing 737-800, Dash 8-Q400 and Dash 8-Q200.

What you will be able to do

  • Explain hot-and-high performance effects and limits
  • Operate engines, APU and systems within limits in hot weather
  • Manage fuel density, uplift and MEL fuel items
  • Handle sand, dust, gusts and summer convective weather
  • Prevent the selected flight safety events
  • Prepare the summer recurrent simulator session
