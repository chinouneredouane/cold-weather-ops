Domestic Airlines — Hot Weather Operations
Lesson 11: Flight Safety Trends — Summer Season

Learning objectives:
- Review the flight safety events typical of the summer season.
- Apply the prevention for each event.
- Report events through the safety reporting system.

Summer focus items

Event Why it matters Prevention

Runway excursion High ground speed, long float, tailwind, sand on the runway Stabilised approach to the aiming point; go around if a long landing develops

High taxi speed Hot brakes, reduced margins, passenger injury Company taxi speeds; short firm braking, never riding the brakes

Wrong flap, ZFW, fuel or temperature entries Wrong speeds and trim: tail strike, degraded take-off Independent cross-check by both pilots

Hard and bounced landings Structural damage, injuries Correct flare; bounced-landing technique; go around if in doubt; report

Turbulence and windshear near convective weather Injuries, loss of control Wide avoidance; seat belts on; windshear escape when required

Use of reverse and APU Company reminder note Apply the reminder note exactly as published

Reporting

Report events and near-misses through the safety reporting system, under a just culture.

Read the latest company safety notes and discuss them with the TRI/TRE before the summer simulator session (mandatory exercises: upset recovery, windshear, TCAS, EGPWS, pilot incapacitation).

Key takeaways:
- Runway excursions: stabilised approach, go around from a long landing.
- Moderate taxi speeds; cross-check flap, ZFW, fuel and temperature entries.
- Avoid convective weather widely; report every event.
