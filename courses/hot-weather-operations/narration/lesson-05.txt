Domestic Airlines — Hot Weather Operations
Lesson 5: Summer Flight Safety Focus Items

Learning objectives:
- Review the flight safety events selected for this season.
- Apply the correct technique to prevent each event.
- Report events through the safety reporting system.

The training department has selected the following flight safety items for discussion with the TRI/TRE:

Event Why it matters Prevention

High taxi speed Hot brakes, reduced margins on turns and runway crossings, passenger injury. Respect the company taxi speeds; use idle thrust and brief, firm braking applications rather than riding the brakes.

Late or long flare Each second of float at high ground speed uses a large part of the runway and increases the landing distance. Fly a stabilised approach to the aiming point; flare at the normal height; go around if a long landing develops.

Incorrect flap setting, ZFW or fuel entries Wrong take-off speeds and trim — tail strike or degraded take-off performance. Independent cross-check of performance data, FMS / performance entries and the load sheet by both pilots.

Hard landing Structural damage, injuries; often after an unstable approach or late flare. Stabilised approach; correct flare; report any suspected hard landing in the technical log.

Bounced landing Risk of tail strike, nose gear damage or a second hard landing. Apply the company bounced landing recovery technique; go around if in doubt.

Use of reverse and APU Covered by the company reminder note (note de rappel). Apply the reminder note exactly as published.

Safety notes and reporting

Read and discuss the latest company safety notes before the simulator session.

Report events through the safety reporting system. Reports are used to improve training and procedures, under a just culture.

Key takeaways:
- Moderate taxi speeds protect brakes and people.
- A late or long flare at high ground speed eats runway — go around if needed.
- Cross-check flap, ZFW and fuel entries.
- Apply the bounced and hard landing techniques; report every event.
