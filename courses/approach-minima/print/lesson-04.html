Module 1 · Approach Operations

Lesson 04: Low-Visibility Operations Overview

Lesson 4 of 4 · about 6 min

Learning objectives

  • Describe the requirements for CAT II and CAT III operations.
  • Explain low-visibility procedures at aerodromes.
  • Recognise when LVO capability is lost.

Requirements

  • A specific approval (SPA.LVO) for the operator, with the aircraft certified, the crew trained and qualified, and procedures in the Operations Manual.
  • The aerodrome with low-visibility procedures (LVP) in force, and the required ground equipment serviceable.
  • The aircraft equipment required for the category serviceable — check the MEL and the AFM.

Low-visibility procedures

When LVP are in force, ATC protects the ILS sensitive and critical areas, increases spacing and uses specific taxi routes. Low-visibility take-off (LVTO) below the standard take-off minima also needs approval and suitable runway lighting.

Loss of capability

  • Aircraft failures (autopilot channels, autothrottle, displays) can downgrade the approach category — the FCOM / AOM gives the effect.
  • Ground equipment failures can also raise the minima — see the next course on inoperative ground equipment.
  • Fog: know the types (radiation, advection) and their trends for planning.
Company authorisation

The approach operations each fleet is authorised for — for example CAT I only, or CAT II for a fleet and crew with the approval — are listed in the AOC operations specifications issued by the DACM. The method and values for minima are in OM A; the aerodrome minima are in OM C and the charts.

Key takeaways

  • CAT II / III: approval, aircraft, crew, procedures, aerodrome LVP and equipment.
  • LVP protect ILS areas and increase spacing.
  • Aircraft or ground failures can downgrade the category.

Knowledge check

Which is NOT required to fly a CAT II approach?

  1. Operator specific approval
  2. LVP in force at the aerodrome
  3. Trained and qualified crew
  4. A weather radar image of the fog

Answer: D. A weather radar image is not a requirement.
