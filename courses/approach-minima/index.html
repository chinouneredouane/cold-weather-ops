Approach Operations and Minima

Classification of approach operations (2D/3D, Type A/B, CAT I–III), aerodrome operating minima, ceiling and visibility, the approach ban and LVO.

Purpose

Refresh all flight crews' knowledge of the approach operations authorised by the authority and of ceiling and visibility requirements, in line with ICAO Annex 6 / Doc 9365 and EASA CAT.OP.MPA.110 and .305.

Who should take this course

All flight crew: Boeing 737-800, Dash 8-Q400 and Dash 8-Q200.

What you will be able to do

  • Classify approach operations
  • Apply aerodrome operating minima, ceiling and visibility
  • Apply planning minima and the approach ban
  • Describe LVO requirements and loss of capability
