Dash 8-Q200 Performance

Dash 8-Q200 performance through all phases of flight with the AFM charts and Operating Data Manual tables: definitions, take-off, climb, cruise, descent, landing, go-around, icing, brake energy and abnormal situations.

Purpose

Enable Q200 flight crew to compute aircraft performance during all phases of flight using the AFM charts, Operating Data Manual tables and related material, in line with EASA CS-25 and Part-CAT.

Who should take this course

Dash 8-Q200 flight crew (type rating and recurrent).

What you will be able to do

  • Explain the environmental factors and definitions
  • Compute take-off speeds, WAT limit, gradients and field lengths
  • Use the climb, cruise and descent tables
  • Compute landing weight limits and go-around gradients
  • Apply icing corrections
  • Respect brake energy and turnaround time
  • Compute abnormal landing distances
