Lesson 5: Take-off Climb Gradients and Field Lengths

Learning objectives:
- Find the second-segment and final-segment gradients with one engine inoperative.
- Find the take-off run, take-off distance and accelerate-stop distance required.
- Apply wind and slope corrections.

Method Compute each requirement, correct for wind and slope, compare with TORA, TODA and ASDA. The lowest weight from field length, WAT, obstacles, brake energy and structure is the maximum take-off weight.

Key takeaways:
- 2nd segment ≥ 2.4 %, final ≥ 1.2 % gross (one engine inoperative).
- TOR, TOD and ASD are read at zero wind/slope, then corrected.
- The most limiting weight applies.
