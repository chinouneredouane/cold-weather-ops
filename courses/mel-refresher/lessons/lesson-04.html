Lesson 04: Failures After Dispatch and Good Practice

Learning objectives

  • Handle failures after the MEL no longer applies.
  • Report defects correctly.
  • Avoid common MEL errors.

Failures after dispatch

  • After the point defined in the MEL preamble, use the QRH / FCOM / AOM non-normal procedures.
  • The decision to continue, return or divert is taken by the commander, based on the AFM limitations, the procedures and the situation — the MEL can help to assess the consequences, but does not authorise continuation by itself.
  • Record every defect in the technical log after the flight.

Common errors

  • Dispatching with an expired rectification interval.
  • Forgetting the (O) procedure — for example a fuel or performance penalty.
  • Misreading “number required”.
  • Not checking the effect on RVSM, PBN or LVO capability.
  • Not informing the other crew member of the restrictions.
Company MEL

Always use the current Domestic Airlines MEL for your fleet (e.g. MEL Ed.02) and its preamble. This course explains the principles; the MEL decides.

Key takeaways

  • After the MEL point, use QRH / FCOM / AOM and commander's judgment.
  • Record every defect in the technical log.
  • Watch expiry dates, (O) procedures, number required and capability effects.
