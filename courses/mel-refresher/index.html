Purpose

Refresh all flight crews' knowledge of the MEL principles in line with ICAO Annex 6 and EASA ORO.MLR.105, and of the correct use of the company MEL.

Who should take this course

All flight crew: Boeing 737-800, Dash 8-Q400 and Dash 8-Q200.

What you will be able to do

  • Explain the MMEL, MEL and CDL
  • Read an MEL item and its rectification category
  • Apply the MEL dispatch process
  • Handle failures after dispatch and avoid common errors
