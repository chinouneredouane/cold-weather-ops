CFIT and EGPWS Refresher

Controlled flight into terrain, an ICAO global high-risk category: data and scenarios, altimetry, CDFA, EGPWS modes and the escape manoeuvre, human factors and case studies, and the interactive FSF CFIT risk checklist.

Purpose

Prevent CFIT in Domestic Airlines operations: ICAO GASP (Doc 10004) priorities, the IATA CFIT study and implementation plan, the Flight Safety Foundation CFIT Checklist and the company SOPs and EGPWS procedures for each fleet.

Who should take this course

All flight crew: Boeing 737-800, Dash 8-Q400 and Dash 8-Q200.

What you will be able to do

  • Explain why CFIT is a global high-risk category and what the data show
  • Recognise CFIT scenarios, contributing factors and precursors
  • Apply altimeter, safe altitude, briefing and CDFA procedures
  • Explain the EGPWS system, modes and enhanced functions
  • Respond immediately and correctly to EGPWS warnings
  • Analyse CFIT accidents with human factors models
  • Assess CFIT risk with the FSF checklist
