Controlled flight into terrain: altimetry, safe altitudes, stabilised approaches and CDFA, EGPWS alerts and the escape manoeuvre for each fleet.

Purpose

Refresh flight crew knowledge of CFIT prevention and EGPWS (TAWS) procedures, in line with ICAO Annex 6 and PANS-OPS, EASA CAT.IDE.A.150 and CAT.OP.MPA.115, and the company SOPs.

Who should take this course

All flight crew: Boeing 737-800, Dash 8-Q400 and Dash 8-Q200.

What you will be able to do

  • Define CFIT and its contributing factors
  • Apply altimeter, safe altitude and chart procedures
  • Challenge clearances and manage complacency
  • Fly stabilised approaches and CDFA
  • Respond correctly to EGPWS cautions and warnings
  • Apply the escape manoeuvre for your fleet
