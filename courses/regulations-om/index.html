Purpose

Refresh all flight crews' knowledge of the regulatory framework and the structure and use of the Operations Manual (MANEX).

Who should take this course

All flight crew: Boeing 737-800, Dash 8-Q400 and Dash 8-Q200.

What you will be able to do

  • Describe the ICAO framework and SARPs
  • Describe the EASA Air Operations structure and national oversight
  • Use the Operations Manual parts A to D
  • Apply the commander's authority and the key OM A rules
