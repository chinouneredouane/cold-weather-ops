Module 1 · Inoperative Ground Equipment

Lesson 02: The Equipment and the Table

Lesson 2 of 3 · about 7 min

Learning objectives

  • Identify the ground equipment covered by the table.
  • Understand approach lighting classes.
  • Read the failed equipment table correctly.

Equipment covered

  • ILS / GLS components — localiser, glide path, standby transmitters, outer and middle markers or the DME replacing them.
  • Navigation aids for non-precision approaches — VOR, NDB, DME.
  • RVR assessment systems — touchdown zone, mid-point and stop-end sensors.
  • Lighting — approach lights, runway edge, threshold and end lights, runway centreline lights, touchdown zone lights, and standby power for lighting.

Approach lighting classes (EASA)

ClassLength of the approach lighting
FALS — full approach lighting720 m or more
IALS — intermediate420 m to 719 m
BALS — basic210 m to 419 m
NALS — no approach lightingless than 210 m, or none

The minimum RVR for an approach depends on the lighting class. When part of the approach lighting fails, the class can change — and the RVR minimum increases.

Reading the table

  1. Find the failed or downgraded item.
  2. Find the column for your type of approach (e.g. CAT II/III, CAT I, 2D).
  3. Read the effect: no effect, a modified condition, an increased RVR, or “not allowed”.
  4. Apply only one entry per failed item; if several items have failed, apply the most restrictive result, and check the notes in the table.
Company table

The effect of each failed or downgraded item on the minima is given in the “failed or downgraded equipment” table of the Domestic Airlines OM A. Use that table — this course explains how it works, not its values.

Key takeaways

  • Covered: ILS components, navaids, RVR systems, lighting.
  • Lighting classes FALS / IALS / BALS / NALS set the RVR minimum.
  • Read the item, the approach type column and the effect; apply the most restrictive result for multiple failures.

Knowledge check

Approach lights 450 m long belong to which class?

  1. FALS
  2. IALS
  3. BALS
  4. NALS

Answer: B. IALS: 420 m to 719 m.
