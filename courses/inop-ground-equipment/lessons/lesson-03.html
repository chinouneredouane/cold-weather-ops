Lesson 03: Applying the Allowances in Flight

Lesson narration

Learning objectives

  • Apply the table during planning and in flight.
  • Brief the revised minima.
  • Handle a failure during the approach.

Planning

  • Check the NOTAMs for the destination and alternates; apply the table to calculate the revised minima.
  • Check that the weather allows the revised minima, with the planning minima for alternates.

In flight

  • Get the ATIS, confirm the status of aids and lighting, recalculate the minima and brief them.
  • If a failure is reported during the approach, assess the effect: on a precision approach, a failure of the guidance usually requires a go-around unless the required visual reference is already established, as your procedure states.
  • Do not improvise minima — if in doubt, use the more restrictive value or go around.

Reporting

Report any observed failure of aids or lighting to ATC; it may not have been detected on the ground.

Company table

The effect of each failed or downgraded item on the minima is given in the “failed or downgraded equipment” table of the Domestic Airlines OM A. Use that table — this course explains how it works, not its values.

Key takeaways

  • Apply the table at planning and again in flight with the latest ATIS.
  • Brief the revised minima.
  • If in doubt, use the more restrictive value or go around; report failures to ATC.

Knowledge check

During an ILS approach, ATC reports the glide path unserviceable above the minimum. What do you do?
