Domestic Airlines — CRM/TEM — Initial and Recurrent
Lesson 12: Assessing Non-Technical Skills (NOTECHS)

Learning objectives:
- Describe the four NOTECHS categories and their elements.
- Use the rating scale of the company form (OM D 2.6.68).
- Explain the observation and feedback cycle.

The four categories

Tap a card to turn it over.
Co-operation Team building and maintaining · consideration of others · support of others · conflict solving. Leadership and managerial skills Use of authority and assertiveness · providing and maintaining standards · planning and co-ordination · workload management. Situation awareness Awareness of aircraft systems · of the external environment · of time. Decision making Problem definition and diagnosis · option generation · risk assessment and option selection.

Principles of assessment

Only observable behaviour is rated — not personality or attitude.

A rating must be linked to a technical consequence to be "unacceptable".

Repeated behaviour is needed to conclude a problem.

The behaviour must be explained in the debriefing.

In the company Recurrent CRM/TEM elements are not assessed; non-technical skills are observed by TRI/TRE in simulator and line checks, and the recurrent programme is updated from these crew assessments (OM D 2.4.5).

Key takeaways:
- NOTECHS: co-operation, leadership/managerial, situation awareness, decision making.
- Rate observable behaviour, with the technical consequence.
- Observations update the training — a continuous cycle.
