CRM/TEM — Initial and Recurrent

Crew Resource Management and Threat and Error Management following the OM D: human error, safety culture, the TEM model with interactive threat/error/UAS exercises, situation awareness, decision making, communication, automation, NOTECHS and case studies.

Purpose

Deliver the company CRM/TEM training of OM D 1.5 (initial 12 h, DCC 6 h, command 9 h) and 2.4.5 (recurrent 6 h, all elements in depth over a 3-year cycle) in e-learning form, to increase crew communication and management skills and the practical use of TEM.

Who should take this course

Flight crew of all fleets (B737-800, Q400, Q200); cabin crew and operations staff for the common elements.

What you will be able to do

  • Explain human error and the error chain
  • Describe safety culture and organisational factors
  • Apply the TEM model: threats, errors, undesired aircraft states, countermeasures
  • Maintain situation awareness and manage workload
  • Make structured decisions under stress and fatigue
  • Communicate, lead and co-operate as a crew
  • Use automation according to the company philosophy
  • Understand NOTECHS assessment
  • Analyse case studies with TEM
