LMS Packages (SCORM 1.2)

Each academy course is available as a SCORM 1.2 package for import into the company LMS (Moodle, Cornerstone, SAP SuccessFactors, TalentLMS and most others). In the LMS, pilots log in with their LMS account; the LMS administrator creates users, manages passwords and sees each pilot's progress, quiz score and completion.

What the LMS receives

  • Status: incomplete while the pilot studies; passed or failed after the final quiz (mastery score = pass mark).
  • Score: the best final quiz result, 0–100.
  • Bookmark and progress: the last lesson and the lessons completed — the pilot resumes where they stopped.
  • Name: the pilot's name from the LMS account is printed on the course certificate.

How to import

  1. Download the package (.zip). Do not unzip it.
  2. In the LMS, create a new course or activity of type SCORM package and upload the zip.
  3. Set the display to open in a new window or full screen, and the grading to “highest attempt”.
  4. Enrol the pilots or their groups (B737-800, Q400, Q200).

The Command Upgrade course has its own design and progress system and is not included as a SCORM package yet.
